Gravando Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

PortuguêsEnglishEspañol

This policy explains how Gravando (Android package com.gravando.app) handles data to record videos, organize takes and provide a teleprompter. Michael Welbert is responsible for the application. Gravando does not require its own user account.

1. Camera, microphone and content

With your permission, the camera and microphone record video and audio, detect speech and recognize commands. Scripts, scenes, takes, videos, preferences and session information support recording, the teleprompter, organization and video assembly. Voice processing and script language identification take place on your device. Gravando does not send audio, videos or scripts to its own servers or to AdMob. You can revoke permissions in Android settings; features that require them will stop working.

2. Storage and export

Session content and preferences are stored on your device. Gravando does not provide an account or its own server to synchronize this content, and disables automatic Android backup of its private app data. Exported videos are saved to the destination you choose or to your device’s video storage. Sharing delivers a file to the app or recipient you select; their privacy practices then apply to that copy. Gallery apps or backup services you choose may synchronize exported files according to their own settings.

3. Advertising and data handled by Google

Versions with advertising use Google AdMob and Google Mobile Ads, with Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable consent flows. The SDK handles IP addresses, product interactions, diagnostics and device/account identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. See the official Google Mobile Ads disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy. This technical processing is separate from the content of your recordings. We do not sell scripts, audio or videos, or use our own behavioral analytics tools.

4. Consent and choices

Where applicable, the app presents consent choices before requesting ads. Ad privacy choices can be reviewed through the option available in the More area when required by Google’s consent system. You can also manage your advertising identifier in Android settings. Choices and ad availability depend on your region, settings and Google’s rules. Lack of consent does not authorize sending your recordings for advertising.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data is retained as needed for sessions and while you keep it. Use library controls to delete scripts and sessions. Deleting a session removes its takes, temporary files and linked final video when the destination is accessible; permission failures may require a retry. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes private local data. Unlinked exports, shared copies and backups held by other services must be deleted at their respective destinations. We do not hold server copies of these recordings to delete remotely. Google’s retention of technical data follows its own policies and controls.

6. Contact and rights

For privacy questions and requests, contact Michael Welbert at michael.welbert.contato@gmail.com. If you send an email, your address and message will be used to respond and handle your request for as long as necessary for that purpose and applicable legal obligations. Avoid unnecessary recordings or sensitive information as attachments. You may request information, access, correction or deletion of information you have sent through this contact. For local content, use the app and Android controls; for data handled by Google, consult the controls described in Google’s policy.

7. Security

Private storage uses Android’s protection mechanisms. Sharing takes place through actions you choose. No device or storage method provides absolute security; keep your device protected and your operating system updated.

8. Privacy of this website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages and includes no first-party cookies, forms, advertising scripts or analytics tools. When you visit, GitHub may process technical connection data, including your IP address, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links and email involve the practices of the respective services.

9. Policy changes

This policy will be revised when the app’s practices change. The update date appears at the top of the page. Changes requiring consent will be handled through the applicable app flow.
